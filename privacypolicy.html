Privacy Policy

Thusfar for Windows · Effective October 26, 2026

The Short Version

  • Your financial records stay on your PC, in a folder you can see and choose.
  • Thusfar has no code that connects to the internet or any other network. It never connects to a bank and needs no account or sign-in.
  • Thusfar collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere: no analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising, no crash reports.
  • We only ever see information you choose to send us yourself, such as a support email.

Who We Are

Thusfar is published by Stanley Fuhriman (“we”, “us”) and distributed through the Microsoft Store. This policy explains what information Thusfar handles, where it is kept and who can see it. It applies to Thusfar for Windows and to this page.

What Thusfar Keeps on Your PC

Everything Thusfar stores is kept in its data folder, Documents\Thusfar unless you choose another folder in Settings. Settings shows where it is.

Your records spending.sqlite
The accounts and account transactions you import, and what you add to them: categories, rules, merchants, notes and corrections. It also records the name of each file you imported, a history of the changes you make, and the date you accepted the license agreement.
Backups backups folder
Copies of your records that Thusfar makes before an upgrade or a deletion, and any you make with Back Up Now.
Settings settings.json
Your preferences, such as the theme and which table columns are shown.
Diagnostics log logs folder
A small log of what the app did, used to troubleshoot problems: event names, counts, timings and the type of any error. It never holds a transaction description, amount, account name or number, or folder path. It stays on your PC.
Folder location
A small file in the app’s own Windows folder that remembers where your data folder is.

Files you export, such as CSV files, are saved wherever you choose. Thusfar reads the files you import but does not keep a copy of them.

What Thusfar Never Does

  • Connect to the internet, to us, to your bank or to any other service. Its Windows package asks for no network access.
  • Ask for bank logins, passwords or a Thusfar account.
  • Collect analytics, usage statistics, device identifiers or crash reports, or show advertising.
  • Sell, rent or share your information. We never receive it, so there is nothing to sell.

When You Contact Us

Settings, Support offers Save Diagnostics, which saves a plain-text file to a place you choose. It holds the app, Windows and database versions, counts of your accounts, transactions, imports, rules and merchants, and the last two diagnostics logs. It is built to leave out descriptions, amounts, account names and folder paths. Thusfar never sends it. If you want help, you can read it, remove anything you would rather not share, and attach it to an email yourself.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you, fix problems and keep a record of support requests. We keep them only as long as needed for that, protect them with reasonable care, and never sell them or use them for advertising. Ask us and we will delete them.

Microsoft, Windows and the Store

The Microsoft Store handles finding, buying, installing and updating Thusfar. If you buy Thusfar, Microsoft processes the purchase, and we never receive your payment details. Microsoft collects information for these services under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Depending on your diagnostic data settings, Windows may send Microsoft an error report if Thusfar stops unexpectedly. Microsoft gives publishers summary reports drawn from what Windows and the Store collect: installs, ratings and reviews, and failure details that help us fix crashes. Thusfar itself sends none of this. You can change what Windows shares in Settings, Privacy & security, Diagnostics & feedback.

If you write a review in the Store, we can read it and may reply publicly.

Keeping Your Records Safe

  • Your records and backups are stored without encryption, so anyone who can open your Windows account or the data folder can read them. Turn on Device encryption or BitLocker in Windows to protect them, and keep backup copies somewhere safe.
  • If your data folder is in a folder that OneDrive or another cloud service syncs, that service copies it to its cloud under its own terms. Thusfar warns you in Settings when the data folder is inside OneDrive.
  • Other software on your PC, such as antivirus or backup tools, may read or copy files in the data folder. That is outside Thusfar’s control.

Your Choices

Your records belong to you and are always on your PC. You can view, correct, export or delete them in Thusfar at any time, or delete the data folder yourself. Uninstalling Thusfar removes the app and its own Windows folder but leaves your data folder, so delete that folder too if you want your records gone.

Because Thusfar does not send us your personal information, we hold none of it to access, correct, delete or transfer, apart from anything you have emailed us. For that, write to us at the address below.

Children

Thusfar is meant for adults: its license agreement requires users to be at least 18, or the age of majority where they live. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

This Page

Changes to This Policy

If the way Thusfar handles information changes, we will update this page and its effective date before releasing the version with that change.
