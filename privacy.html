BudgetKind

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

BudgetKind keeps your budget on your PC. It has no sign-in, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

BudgetKind is a personal budgeting app for Windows made by Stanley Fuhriman. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What BudgetKind keeps

The accounts, transactions, bills, budgets and settings you enter or import are saved in an encrypted file on your PC, in the app's own folder. Windows protects the key to that file, so only your Windows user account on that PC can open it.

What BudgetKind sends

Nothing. BudgetKind does not need an internet connection, and it does not send your budget, your files or information about how you use the app to the developer or to anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Files you choose to save

  • Backups are saved only where you choose. You can protect a backup with a password.
  • Exports are saved only where you choose. An export is not encrypted, and the app tells you so before you save one.
  • Support files hold technical details, like when the app started or what kind of error happened. They never hold names, amounts, notes or file paths. The app keeps a small log of these details on your PC. It leaves your PC only if you save a support file and send it to someone yourself.

Removing your data

Uninstalling BudgetKind removes its folder and your budget from your PC. Make a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Microsoft Store

If you get BudgetKind from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, install and updates. Microsoft's privacy statement covers that, not this policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
